## Pictures made with an AI image tool

These were generated by the Salvage Rights team with Nano Banana 2 on 2026-11-24. We will replace any of them as
soon as someone gives us work made by a person that we can legally use (see the README).

- Site icon (`public/favicon-32.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`, `public/icon-512.png`): cropped and resized.
  Prompt: "Site icon and logo mark: a simple emblem that reads at 32 px, such as a crane hook, a salvage tag or a
  wrench over a 'Mech silhouette. Flat, two or three colours, no fine detail. The site currently has no icon of its
  own."
- Share card (`.github/social-preview.jpg` in the repository root): the generated title was painted out and the
  project name and tagline were set in its place; resized to 1280 by 640.
  Prompt: "I am going to tell what I need the image for... GitHub social preview: the card shown when the
  repository link is shared. 1280×640, with the main subject in the middle and room for the project name. A wider
  cut of the salvage yard would do."
